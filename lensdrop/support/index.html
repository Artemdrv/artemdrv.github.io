Updated: September 20, 2026

Help with glasses connectivity, diagnostics and files stored in LensDrop on iPhone.

Create a diagnostic report

  1. Open LensDrop diagnostics from the welcome screen and run the check.
  2. Wait for the result. If a stage cannot complete, later stages remain marked as not run.
  3. Create a support report. Use the system share sheet to save the ZIP to Files or send it to the email address above.

You can run diagnostics and create a report without connected glasses. Reports are not sent automatically: you choose the recipient.

What to include

Include your iPhone model, iOS version, LensDrop version and build number, glasses model, steps taken and actual result. Attach the ZIP created by the app; photos and videos from your glasses are not needed for routine diagnostics. Remove unrelated personal information from screenshots. Do not send passwords, tokens or keys.

If you see IDENTITY_NOT_AVAILABLE

In build 0.4.1 (5), reading the glasses’ existing authorization is not integrated yet. The check therefore stops with IDENTITY_NOT_AVAILABLE and real import does not start. This is a known limitation of the build.

Do not reset your glasses, remove their registration or change ownership to address this error. Save the report and contact support. Demo mode lets you explore the interface; it does not confirm a connection to real glasses.

Permissions and saved files

  • Bluetooth access is used for device discovery. Check LensDrop’s permission in iOS Settings.
  • LensDrop’s library contains files stored inside the app, not your entire system Photos library.
  • Enabling export to Photos requests add-only access. In demo mode, the exported items are demonstration images and videos.
  • After export to Photos, further synchronization depends on your iCloud Photos settings.

Before removing the app

Export any files you need through Share or save them to Photos first. LensDrop’s primary local storage is excluded from device backups. There is no developer-operated cloud recovery service. Do not reinstall the app as a troubleshooting step while it still contains files you need.

See the Privacy Policy for details about data handling.
