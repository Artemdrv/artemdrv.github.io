Privacy Policy

Updated: September 20, 2026

This policy describes data handling in LensDrop for iPhone and on its support pages. The developer is ARTSEM DARAFEYEU (Artem Dorofeev). It covers the current app version, 0.4.1.

LensDrop does not automatically send your photos, videos or usage history to the developer. The app has no proprietary account system, advertising SDKs, third-party analytics or server for storing user files.

Data on your iPhone

The app stores settings, the initial catalog baseline, media information, import state, saved files and incomplete downloads locally. Demo media is bundled with the app. LensDrop’s primary storage and temporary diagnostic ZIP files are excluded from device backups; there is no developer-operated cloud recovery service.

Import from real glasses is not yet available in version 0.4.1 (5): the check stops at authorization. Demo files are not presented as data received from your glasses.

Bluetooth and local connectivity

The app uses system Bluetooth features for device discovery and checks the availability of local connectivity to glasses. iOS controls permissions. Connection state and diagnostic results are processed on the device and are not automatically sent to the developer.

Photos, export and other apps

When exporting to the system Photos app, LensDrop requests add-only access. It does not read or delete your existing Photos library. After export, iCloud Photos may synchronize files according to your Apple settings.

Using Share passes the selected file to the app or recipient you choose. That service’s policies govern subsequent handling. LensDrop does not upload these files to a developer-operated server.

Diagnostic reports

You can create a ZIP containing the app version and build, iPhone model, iOS version, connectivity capabilities, permission states, error codes, check stages, relative durations and media counters. The report excludes photos and videos, glasses names and identifiers, network addresses, keys, tokens, Keychain contents and raw error messages. Creating a report does not itself send it to the developer.

Support requests

If you write to drv.8604@gmail.com, the developer receives your email address, message and attachments you choose to send. These are used to reply and investigate the issue and retained as needed to resolve the request or meet applicable obligations. Email is processed by the relevant email providers. Use the same address for questions about these records or requests to delete them.

Apple services

App Store, TestFlight, system diagnostics and iCloud operate under Apple’s policies. Depending on the service and your settings, Apple may provide the developer with test feedback, crash information and other diagnostics. This is separate from LensDrop’s local ZIP report. See Apple’s Privacy Policy.

This website

These pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. We add no analytics scripts, advertising, cookies or tracking pixels. GitHub processes technical request information, including IP addresses, as part of operating its service under the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Managing your data

You control permissions in iOS Settings and choose which files to export. Save files you need before deleting LensDrop: removing the app deletes its local storage. Copies already exported to Photos, Files or other services are managed separately. The developer cannot remotely recover your local app contents.

LensDrop does not intentionally request children’s personal information. Contact the developer at the email above with privacy questions or requests concerning applicable rights.

Policy changes

This page will be updated with a new date if data handling changes. Additional notice or consent will be provided when applicable rules require it.
