Privacy Policy for Flap Sheet

Effective date: September 3, 2026

Flap Sheet respects your privacy. The app does not require a user account and does not collect, sell, rent, or share personal information.

Data Collection

Flap Sheet does not collect personal data from users. It does not use third-party analytics, advertising SDKs, or tracking technologies.

Local Device Data

Flap Sheet stores app settings, performance content, and related preferences locally on the device using Apple system storage. This information remains on the device and is not transmitted to the developer.

Motion Data

Some Flap Sheet functionality uses motion sensors through Apple’s Core Motion framework. Motion information is processed on the device only for the app’s performance controls and is not stored on external servers or transmitted to the developer.

System Volume

The app may read the device’s current system output volume to support performance controls. Flap Sheet does not transmit or use this information for tracking, analytics, or advertising.

Network and Third Parties

Flap Sheet does not use external application servers for its core functionality and does not include third-party advertising or analytics services.

Children’s Privacy

Flap Sheet does not knowingly collect personal information from children or other users because the app does not collect personal information.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if the app’s functionality or data practices change. The current version will be published at this URL.
