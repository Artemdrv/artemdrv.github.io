Privacy Policy for Flash Charge

Effective date: September 4, 2026

Flash Charge respects your privacy. The app does not require a user account and does not collect, sell, rent, or share personal information.

Data Collection

Flash Charge does not collect personal data from users. It does not use third-party analytics, advertising SDKs, tracking technologies, or external telemetry services.

Local Device Data

Flash Charge stores app settings and preferences locally on the device, including selected language, performance settings, visual customization, carrier text, and related configuration. This information remains on the device and is not transmitted to the developer.

Wallpaper Images

If you select an image to use as a wallpaper inside Flash Charge, that image is used locally on your device for the simulated Lock Screen. Flash Charge does not upload the selected image to the developer or to external servers.

Battery Information

Flash Charge may read the device battery level and charging state through Apple system APIs to create its visual charging effect. Battery information is processed on the device and is not transmitted to the developer.

Motion Data

Some Flash Charge performance modes use motion sensors through Apple’s Core Motion framework. Motion information is processed on the device only to support the app’s performance controls and is not stored on external servers or transmitted to the developer.

Network and Third Parties

Flash Charge does not use external application servers for its core functionality and does not include third-party advertising, analytics, tracking, or account services.

Children’s Privacy

Flash Charge does not knowingly collect personal information from children or other users because the app does not collect personal information.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if the app’s functionality or data practices change. The current version will be published at this URL.
