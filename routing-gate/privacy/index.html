Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 13, 2026

This policy explains how Routing Gate for iPhone handles information. The app is provided by ARTSEM DARAFEYEU (Artem Dorofeev), referred to here as “the developer”.

The app does not send your Gates, Remotes, images or usage activity to the developer. It has no developer account system, advertising SDK or third-party analytics service.

Information stored on your device

Routing Gate stores Spaces, Gates, their states, Remotes, button actions, Shortcut names, links, selected images and preferences in the app’s local storage. Language and navigation preferences also use Apple’s local settings storage. Images you select through Photos or Files are processed and stored on your device; the app does not upload them to the developer.

The app does not provide its own cloud synchronization or an online copy of this content. Your device backup settings and Apple’s services may affect whether local app data is included in a backup.

Seven-day trial

The start date of the trial is stored locally, including in Apple Keychain, to determine the remaining access period. Keychain information may remain after the app is deleted. Reinstalling the app does not necessarily start a new trial. This trial record is not sent to the developer.

Purchases through Apple

Purchases and subscription billing are handled by Apple. Routing Gate uses Apple StoreKit transaction information, such as product and transaction identifiers, dates, purchase status and subscription renewal status, to determine access, restore eligible purchases and calculate qualifying Monthly payments toward Lifetime.

The app does not send that transaction history to a developer-operated server. The developer does not receive your payment-card details through the app. Apple processes purchase and account information under its Privacy Policy and may provide developers with App Store sales or diagnostic reports through Apple’s services.

Shortcuts, links and other apps

You choose the Apple Shortcuts, websites and apps used by your Remote buttons. Running an action can open those services and pass the input or link configured for that action. Their own privacy policies apply to information they receive. Routing Gate does not control what a Shortcut or external destination does with that information.

Support messages

If you email drv.8604@gmail.com, the developer receives your email address, message and any attachments you choose to send. This information is used to answer your request and investigate the issue, and is retained as needed for support or applicable legal obligations. Email is processed by the relevant email providers. Avoid sending passwords, full payment details or unrelated personal information.

This website

These pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. They contain no added advertising, analytics scripts, cookies or tracking pixels. GitHub may process technical request information, including IP addresses, to serve and protect the website, as described in the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Control, deletion and privacy requests

You can edit or delete your content within the app. Deleting the app normally removes its local app data; Keychain trial information may remain, and device backups are managed separately. Restoring purchases does not restore deleted Gates, Remotes or images. The developer cannot remotely retrieve your local content or reset your device’s Keychain.

To ask about information supplied in a support message, request its deletion, or exercise privacy rights available under applicable law, contact drv.8604@gmail.com. The app does not intentionally request personal information from children.

Changes

If the app’s data practices change, this policy will be updated here with a new effective date. Any additional notice or consent required by applicable law will be provided.
